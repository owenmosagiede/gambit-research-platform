Our approach

From signal to portfolio.

A forecast is one part of a portfolio decision. We also consider how positions interact and what it costs to trade them.

Signal research

We look for repeatable relationships between market observations and future returns. Each test records the instrument, forecast horizon, and outcome.

Forecasting

We combine signals that predict the same outcome over the same horizon and evaluate each model on later observations.

Portfolio construction

We study how position sizes, concentration, and turnover affect simulated performance after trading costs.
