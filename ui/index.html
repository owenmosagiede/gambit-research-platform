Exact identities
Feature and Scope require a registered ID and version. Display names are not identifiers. The service checks their compatibility and freezes the selected membership and data references.
One request, five horizons
Price Return includes 1, 7, 21, 63 and 252 scheduled weekdays. There is no separate horizon field. An individual pair selects TS; a currency universe selects CS with a common quote reference.
Request identity
Use a new canonical UUID for a new request. If a response is uncertain, check its saved status and retry with the same UUID and identical inputs. A new UUID does not override duplicate-experiment checks.
Research method
The service chooses and freezes the applicable Research Spec. P1 is discovery; admitted candidates proceed to P2 validation. Acceptance into the queue does not establish a research result.
